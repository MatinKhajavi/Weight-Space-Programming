## Design

**Strategy.** The submarine never moves left or right; the left and right logits are fixed at −20. It therefore stays in column 5, facing left as it does at reset, with its back cell in column 6. Because the column is fixed, every relation the policy needs depends only on the submarine's row, and the network can work with absolute grid positions.

The policy:
- **Hunt and fire.** Fire when a fish, enemy sub or diver is on the left side (columns 0–4) of its own row. Otherwise move up or down toward rows with such targets. Nearer rows weigh more (1.5, 1.2, 1.0, 0.8, …), so targets above and below do not cause oscillation.
- **Flee.** Leave the current row when danger approaches: enemies or bullets in columns 6–8, or enemy bullets in columns 3–4.
- **Never enter a blocked row.** A row is blocked if it has danger in columns 4–8, or enemy bullets in column 3.
- **Divers.** Divers on the left of a row are targets, so the submarine waits in that row until they reach column 5 and are picked up.
- **Surface mode.** This turns on when gauge cell (9,8) is lit and gauge cell (9,2) is dark. That means oxygen is below 60 and at least one diver is on board (oxygen this low cannot light column 8). The submarine then heads up and surfaces; firing and moving down are suppressed, but blocked rows are still avoided.
- **Never surface without a diver.** Moving up from row 1 is allowed only in surface mode, because surfacing with no diver ends the episode.
- **Row 0.** At the surface the submarine dives, unless row 1 is blocked.

**Layer 1 (gated per-row units).** Each unit is gated by the submarine's own cells in row r, (r,5) and (r,6), with weight +12 each and bias −24. So the unit sits at exactly −1 unless the submarine is in that row, and then it computes its feature.
- **H_r** (units 0–7, rows 1–8): "stay and fire" score = left-side targets − danger in the own row.
- **U_r** (units 8–15): "move up" score = own-row danger + targets above (decaying with distance) − 3 × danger level of row r−1.
  - U_1 (unit 8) uses only the surface-mode gauge test.
- **D_r** (units 16–23, rows 0–7): the same as U_r, mirrored downward.
  - D_0 (unit 16) has a +2 bias so the submarine dives from the surface.
- **O1** (unit 24): surface mode, computed as tanh(6·g8 − 6·g2 − 3).

**Layer 2.** For each family, the sum over rows plus 7 equals the submarine's own-row value, since every other row contributes −1.
- **UP2** = tanh(4·U_mine + 2.5·S), where S is surface mode.
- **DN2** = tanh(4·D_mine − 3.5·S).
- **H2** = tanh(4·H_mine − 6·S).

**Output logits.** up = 4·UP2, down = 4·DN2, fire = 6·H2 + 1, no-op = 0, left = right = −20.
