## Design

**Strategy (hand-derived from the game code).** The paddle only needs to be at the ball's column (or its next column) when the ball goes from row 8 to row 9. My policy has four exclusive branches, selected by what the ball/trail pair looks like:

1. **Certain descent, diagonal trail** (ball row ≥ 3, trail one row up): compute the unfolded landing column u = x + dx·(9−y). This is *linear* in the one‑hot inputs: ball cell (y,x) gets weight x(10−y), trail cell (r,c) gets c(r−8). Landing column = max(−u−1, min(u, 19−u)) (one wall reflection). "Left" ⇔ pos > landing ⇔ [pos+u ≥ 0] ∧ ([pos−u ≥ 1] ∨ [pos+u ≥ 20]); "Right" ⇔ [pos+u ≤ −2] ∨ ([pos−u ≤ −1] ∧ [pos+u ≤ 18]).
2. **Certain descent after a brick bounce at row 3** (ball row 4, trail same row): same logic with u = 6x − 5·trail_x.
3. **Wall column cases** (ball and trail both in column 0 or 9, descending or same-cell at rows 3/4 incl. the initial state): landing = 9−y (col 0) or y (col 9), no reflection needed.
4. **Just bounced off paddle** (ball & trail both on row 8): move toward the midpoint of ball and trail (this correctly handles the horizontal flip when the paddle caught the ball at its new column). Otherwise (**ascending / in bricks**): follow the ball with a 2-cell lead in its true horizontal direction, z = pos − 3x + 2·trail_x (with ±2 corrections at the wall columns where ball and trail share a column), which keeps lead ≥ 0 even after one sticky action at wall reversals.

**Hidden units.** Layer 1 (22 units, tanh(10·(integer ± ½)) so outputs are ±1): follow thresholds Lf/Rf; G (row‑8 bounce) and midpoint thresholds Lg/Rg; DA (descending diagonal, rows ≥ 3) and six thresholds A1,A2,B1,B2,C1,C2 on pos∓u; DB4 (row‑4 same‑row) with six analogous thresholds; DW (wall‑column detector) with LW/RW. Layer 2 (14 units): pure AND gates of layer‑1 signs implementing the formulas above with branch exclusivity. Layer 3: sums the "left" gates into logit 1 and "right" gates into logit 3 (40 when active, 0 otherwise), no‑op bias 20, up/down/fire −100.
