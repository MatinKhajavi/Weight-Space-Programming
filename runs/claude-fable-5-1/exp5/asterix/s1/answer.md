## Design

**Key facts used.** Each of rows 1‑8 holds at most one entity (slot = row). Gold crosses the whole row, so if I simply wait in a gold's row it comes to me. Spawns at x=0 always move right, spawns at x=9 always move left, and the trail cell reveals direction, so from (x, trail) alone I can read "approaching / receding" and the distance to the player's column. I therefore never move horizontally: the player stays at column 5 forever (safe from edge spawns), and all enemy features are lookup tables over (x, direction) relative to column 5.

**Policy (per frame).** Let each row have a danger level e ∈ {−1: nothing/gold/receding enemy, −0.5: approaching enemy 5 cells away, −0.25: 4 away, 0: 3 away, 0.5: 2 away, 1: ≤1 away or on my column}. For moving up from my row r: score = 8·(e_r − e_{r−1}) − 2 + 5·[gold approaching in r−1] − 5·[gold approaching in r] + 3·[gold in r−2] − 1.5·(e_{r−2}+1) (sticky‑action risk) − 1.5 if entering row 1, +3 if leaving row 8 (symmetric for down). Up from row 1 / down from row 8 get −20. No‑op is 0, left/right/fire are −100. So: stay idle, step into (or 2 steps toward) gold rows, leave a row only for a neighbor that is at least half a level safer, prefer gold rows, avoid dead‑end edge rows, and penalize moves whose sticky repeat would land in a lethal row.

**Hidden layer 1 (24 of 32 units, all saturated ±1 except the graded one):**
- units 0‑7: E_r — graded danger level of row r (enemy ch + trail ch lookup solved exactly so that tanh gives −1/−0.5/−0.25/0/0.5/1; gold channel pushes it to −1).
- units 8‑15: G_r — approaching gold in row r (same trick on gold+trail; enemy channel suppresses).
- units 16‑23: P_r — player in row r (one‑hot).

**Hidden layer 2 (16 units):** unit r−1 = "up‑score of row r", unit r+7 = "down‑score of row r": tanh(10·(P_r−1) + 0.02·score). For the player's row this is ≈0.02·score (linear regime); for other rows it is exactly −1, cancelled by the output bias (7·250). Output: logit_up = 250·Σ up‑units + 1750, logit_down likewise, so logit ≈ 5·score.
