**Observations**

*Layer 0 (reads only token+position embeddings).* `W_q.0/W_k.0/W_v.0` are small (std 0.04–0.05, close to the 0.02 init) except two heads; `ln1_w.0` is bimodal (median 0.24, ~20 dims at 0.7–0.98): the 30-dim subspace read by L0H3's Q/K sits on the large-gain dims, the ~20-dim subspace read by L0H3's V on the small-gain dims. These two subspaces are nearly orthogonal (principal cosines ≤0.42); the one read by W_v (call it T, ~17–23 dims) is also read by `W_fc.0` at 26 % of its energy (5× chance) while the other (P, ~30 dims) is avoided by the MLP (6 %, below chance) – so T = character identities, P = positions.
- L0H3: `|W_q|=14.1, |W_k|=14.7`, QK singular values flat (8.9, 8.7, 8.6, 8.5, 8.2 … 13 values ≥6.9), q/k 82–87 % inside P, V 80 % inside T; QK restricted to P has complex eigenvalues of nearly equal modulus (7–8) at angles 35–80° (shift/rotation operator, not a smooth window). Its OV passes 16–17 strong character dimensions downstream (`W_v.1·OV3` sv: 1.96…0.90 then a cliff to 0.4).
- L0H5: QK ≈ 0 (sv 1.44 then ≤0.4 = decayed init), OV enormous (sv 24.8, 12.9) reading the top-2 position directions → running average of the position embedding, written into 4 residual dims (21, 99, 58, 39).
- L0H1/H2/H4: near-duplicates (QK cos 0.42–0.58, OV cos 0.67–0.80, query dirs cos 0.80–0.85), rank-1–3 QK (sv 6.7/4.3/4.1), key direction partly in T (a particular character), OV rank 2 copying the attended token's *position* into the same 4 dims as L0H5 (principal cosines 0.99/0.99/0.90).
- L0H0: QK mixes P (35–40 %) and T (15–17 %); OV copies T (44 %) with mixed-sign eigenvalues.

*Layers 1–3.* All six L1 heads are statistical clones (|q|≈16, QK sv 21–25, OV trace −29…−37, pairwise QK cos 0.21–0.30); L2 the same. K-composition with L0H1/2/4/5 is 5–14× the random baseline for every L1/L2 head and L3H0/1/2/5; with L0H3/L0H0 it is ≈1 everywhere. 34–49 % of each L1 key's energy lies in the 4-dim "position channel"; `ln1_b.1` (outliers −1.57, +1.37, −1.34 on dims 39/58/99) is 70 % in that channel, and the resulting constant query `W_q.1·b1` (norm ≈6 vs ≈1 for ordinary rows) produces a query-independent key preference with loadings (−23, +17) on the channel in every L1 head, (−5…−13, +5…+10) in L2, ≈±2.5 in L3H0/1/2/5, 0 in L3H3/H4. Token-dependent modulation (MLP0-features × channel) is the largest remaining block (16–23 %); the token×token block is strongly negative-definite (same-character/self avoidance). Values of L1 heads read L0H3's output (22 %) and MLP0's output (36–41 %) and avoid the position channel (<6 %). All 18 heads of layers 1–3 have OV matrices that are essentially negative semidefinite (e.g. L1H0: Σpos = 0.7, Σneg = −16; L3H4: 4.3 vs −43): they *subtract* the identities of the characters they attend to. L3H0/1/2/5 additionally erase the position channel (cos(OV·A, A) = −0.5…−0.84 for A = L0H1/2/4/5 outputs; OV diagonal −0.8 on the channel) before the readout. The (current-token × L0H3-prev-token) QK block – the induction signature – holds ≤1 % of any head's energy (random ≈0.3 %); no head anywhere K-composes with the previous-token head. MLP0 has only 601/1536 trained neurons (rows >1) versus all 1536 in later layers.

**Heads**
- L0H0: unclear – QK mixes position and character directions, OV copies character identity with mixed-sign eigenvalues; barely read downstream (L1H4, L3H4 only).
- L0H1: broad – low-rank content key (one character type, shared with H2/H4), attends to all its occurrences and copies their *positions* into the 4-dim position channel (delimiter/line-start feature).
- L0H2: broad – duplicate of H1 (OV cos 0.80).
- L0H3: previous – position-only QK (87 % in P), flat rank-~30 spectrum, shift-like complex eigenvalues; OV copies ~17 character dims; feeds L1 queries/values and MLP1 (the "previous character" channel).
- L0H4: broad – weaker duplicate of H1/H2 (OV cos 0.67–0.70), same key direction.
- L0H5: broad – QK at init level (uniform attention), huge rank-2 OV averaging the position embedding → absolute-position code (dims 21/99/58/39) used as key by 16 later heads.
- L1H0–L1H5: local – all six identical: sharp, query-independent recency/line-position attention via the position channel (loadings −12…−23/+12…+18), avoid positions carrying the same character, values = character identities (L0H3 + MLP0), negative-definite OV → subtract the characters seen in the last few positions / current line. (L1H4 is the weakest copy.)
- L2H0–L2H5: broad – same mechanism with half the loadings (wider windows), same negative-definite subtractive OV; no composition with anything but the position channel.
- L3H0: broad – weak channel loading (≈−2.4/+2.3), OV erases the position channel (cos −0.57…−0.80) = cleanup before readout.
- L3H1: broad – same (erases channel, cos −0.84 with L0H5 output).
- L3H2: broad – same.
- L3H3: unclear – globally positive-definite QK ("attend to positions whose state resembles mine"), ignores the position channel; faint positive (current char × previous-char channel) block ≈1 % energy – far too weak for a real induction head.
- L3H4: broad – tiny QK (sv 3.7), very large negative-definite OV (sv 29): context-wide average subtracted from the residual.
- L3H5: broad – like L3H0/1/2 (channel loading −2.6/+2.1, erases channel, cos −0.75).

**Reasoning**
The network contains exactly one order-sensitive device (the previous-character head L0H3) and nothing that uses it for matching: no K-composition, no copy/induction head, no diverse n-gram heads. Instead layers 1–3 are 16 clones of one head that attends by a position/line-start feature (built by L0H1/2/4/5 from the positions of a delimiter character and the running position mean), reads character identities as values, and subtracts them – a multi-scale "bag of characters seen recently / in the current line, with those characters made less likely". That is the optimal computation when each line is a bag whose letters are drawn without replacement, and it is permutation-invariant within the line; an as-is corpus (code, English or romaji) trained for 30k steps on 1 B characters would certainly have grown induction heads with strong K-composition on L0H3 and specialised heads instead of clones. So the text was line-shuffled. The strong investment in line-boundary machinery (three delimiter-position heads, the newline-dependent channel keying every later head, the recency windows widening from L1 to L3) indicates lines short enough that most 128-character windows contain several newlines – Python source (~35 chars/line) fits best, romaji literature (longer paragraphs) second, Wikipedia paragraphs (hundreds of characters) least; the ~17–20 strongly tracked character dimensions are compatible with all three and do not separate them.
