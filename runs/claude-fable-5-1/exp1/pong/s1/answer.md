**Reasoning**

A game with a vertically moving agent pinned to the left column, horizontal actions that are null, a single moving object that travels through columns 2–8 (never into the agent's column) whose direction of motion is highly informative, no obstacles or collectibles inside the arena, and a permanent reward‑target strip along the right wall is a paddle‑and‑ball game. Grid‑world navigation, Snake, collect‑the‑dots or dodge‑enemies games are ruled out: those would need agent weights across the whole grid and meaningful left/right actions, and would have ch2/ch3 structure in the interior. "Catch" (falling objects) is less likely than Pong because the ball's previous position gets weights as large as its current one (bounce direction matters, not just straight‑line fall), and because the right wall is explicitly tagged as the reward surface – the surface the ball must be sent back to. The ball never being rendered in column 1 fits a Pong rule where contact with the paddle at column 2 reflects it and a miss ends the episode.
